Transfer curve — Vout vs ΔVin

Gain = 100 → the linear region is only about ±6 mV wide. Past that, one transistor takes the whole tail current and the output sits at a rail. This is why op-amps are used inside feedback loops: the loop keeps ΔVin near zero.
